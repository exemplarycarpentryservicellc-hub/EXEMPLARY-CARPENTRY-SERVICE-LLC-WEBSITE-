Privacy Policy

Effective October 6, 2026

Exemplary Carpentry Service ("we", "us") provides finish carpentry, flooring, cabinet, painting, and hardscaping services in Goose Creek, South Carolina, and the surrounding Lowcountry. This policy explains what information we collect through this website, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Information you give us

When you request an estimate through our form, we collect the details you choose to send:

  • your name, phone number, and email address (email is optional);
  • the ZIP code of the property and the type of service you need;
  • any project details you write in the message field.

When you call, text, or email us, we receive your phone number or email address and the content of your message.

How we use it

  • to contact you about your project, schedule an on-site visit, and prepare your written quote;
  • to answer your questions and keep records of the work we discussed or performed;
  • to understand which pages and ads bring people to our website, in aggregate.

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information.

Services that help run this website

  • Web3Forms delivers the estimate form to our inbox. Your form submission passes through their servers to reach us.
  • Google Tag Manager and Google Analytics may record anonymous usage data, such as pages visited, device type, and approximate location, using cookies. The events our website sends never include your name, phone number, or email address.
  • Google Ads may use cookies to measure whether an ad led to a call or a form request.
  • OpenStreetMap provides the map tiles in our service area section. Loading the map sends your IP address to their servers, like any image on the web.

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings, and you can opt out of Google Analytics with the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

How long we keep it

We keep estimate requests and project records for as long as they are useful for your project, warranty questions, or our business and tax records. You can ask us to delete your information at any time, unless we are required to keep it by law.

Your choices

You can ask us what information we have about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. You can also ask us to stop contacting you. Just call or email us using the details below.

Children

This website is meant for adults planning work on a home or property. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the effective date at the top of this page.
